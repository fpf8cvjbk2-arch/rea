# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS in a single file (as the incumbent site). No framework, no build step.

## Users

Two audiences, both reading in Italian, usually on a phone, often arriving from Instagram.
1. People looking for themselves ("in cerca di sé"): they read slowly, at length, alone, in a reflective moment.
2. Potential clients of the practitioner: they judge seriousness, care and trustworthiness from how the writing is presented before deciding to get in touch.

## Product Purpose

"Costellazioni quantiche retrocausali" is a personal journal on family constellations (costellazioni familiari): long-form essays on family dynamics, readings, working notes. Success: the reader finishes an article, feels met and not sold to, and follows the Instagram profile or reaches out.

## Positioning

A journal written from lived practice, with explicit epistemic honesty: every essay closes with a note that the systemic view is a reading key, not a law, and not a substitute for psychotherapy or medical care. Case names are fictional.

## Brand commitments

- Site name: "Costellazioni quantiche retrocausali" (kept verbatim).
- All article text, dates, titles, excerpts and closing disclaimers are preserved word for word.
- Instagram is the only outbound call to action today (https://www.instagram.com/costellazioni_q.r/).
- The footer disclaimer (not a psychotherapy) stays.
